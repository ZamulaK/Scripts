Privacy policy

Christmas Lounge (xmaslounge.pages.dev) · last updated November 4, 2026

Christmas Lounge is a personal tool, made by its owner for their own use. It records which songs SomaFM's Christmas Lounge station plays and compares them with the owner's YouTube playlist. It isn't offered to the public. Access to the site is limited to its owner by Cloudflare Access.

What it uses from your Google account

  • With your permission, the site uses the YouTube Data API, through Google's sign-in, to read the playlist and, when you ask it to on the Shuffle page, change the order of its songs.
  • It doesn't read, change or delete anything else in your YouTube or Google account. It doesn't upload, comment, subscribe or post.
  • To keep you signed in, the site keeps the permission Google gives it (a sign-in token) in the site's own storage on its owner's Cloudflare account. It is used only to make the requests above.

What it keeps

  • The songs SomaFM played (artist, title, album, time), read from SomaFM's public song list.
  • The playlist's song titles and video IDs, and the marks, favorites, categories and notes you add on the site.
  • A count of the YouTube quota used each day.

All of this is kept on the owner's Cloudflare account (Cloudflare Pages, Workers and D1). Your browser keeps only settings for the site, such as the theme.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, and nothing is used for advertising. There is no analytics or tracking. Data goes only to the services the site needs to work: Google (YouTube) and Cloudflare.

The site's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Remove the site's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The site then can no longer reach YouTube until you sign in again.
  • The site's owner can delete everything it keeps from their Cloudflare account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
